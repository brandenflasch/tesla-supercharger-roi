Tesla has pricing configured in its API for 31 markets, but a publicly-launched configurator in only 11 (green, verified Jun 13). The other 20 (violet) return real pricing yet their public page 404s — priced internally, not yet launched. Overwhelmingly European; the U.S. is the only market with per-state pricing and the 50-state view. Saudi Arabia, Qatar & Jordan were announced but return no pricing (amber).

        Disclaimer: Outputs are for indicative purposes only and should not be considered official predictions from Tesla. Tesla makes no guarantee of profitability. Actual results can differ materially. Model assumptions (reverse-engineered from Tesla's ROI API): 13% energy loss grid-to-battery (0.87 multiplier on revenue), 7% YoY utilization growth, $0.10/kWh Tesla operational fee escalating at 3%/yr, flat energy procurement cost (no utility rate escalation), no depreciation, no maintenance, no demand charges, no parking rent, excludes hardware taxes. No discounting (simple cumulative, not NPV).
        Sources & date: Pricing pulled from Tesla's /api/energy/supercharger/pricing endpoint; public-launch status from per-market page checks. All data accurate as of June 13, 2026. International FX via open.er-api.com (live). Note: between the June 4 and June 13 pulls, Tesla corrected its earlier Maine ($0.008/kWh) and Norway (~10×-low hardware) data errors, and revised driver prices down in ~18 not-yet-launched markets.
