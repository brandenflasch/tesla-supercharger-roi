Where Supercharger for Business Is Live

33 markets
Tesla has pricing configured in its API for 33 markets, and a publicly-launched configurator in 29 of them (green, verified Oct 22). The other 4 (violet) return real pricing yet have no public configurator — priced internally, not yet launched. Overwhelmingly European; the U.S. and Canada are the only markets with sub-national pricing, and each has its own state or province view (blue). Saudi Arabia, Qatar & Jordan were announced but return no pricing (amber). Liechtenstein is priced but is too small to render at this map resolution.

        33 priced markets
        Real per-country hardware, install, fee and rate data from Tesla's pricing API, run through the same ROI model as the US analysis (per stall, 8-post site assumed for cabinet sharing). Pricing is national outside the US and Canada, which appear here as the average of their states or provinces. Payback is currency-independent (CapEx and profit scale together), so it compares directly; monetary columns normalize to USD or EUR at live FX, or show each market's native currency.
        Country Hardware/Stall Install CapEx/Stall Tesla Fee Energy Sell Util Profit/Stall/Yr Payback
        Payback is an idealized year-1 figure at Tesla's assumed average utilization — no ramp-up, downtime, demand charges or rent — so real-world is longer everywhere; use it to rank markets, not as a guarantee. Energy/sell/fee are per-kWh.
        Confidence: the international utilization input is weaker than the US one. A cross-check of Tesla's scAvgUtilization against independently measured stall occupancy reproduces Tesla's US per-state values — R² 0.72 across 49 jurisdictions with a zero intercept (1.3 kWh on a mean of 337), which implies a 57.4 kW mean power while a car is plugged. That agrees with Tesla's own published Tesla Diner throughput (52.8 kW) and its fleet session figures (54 kW). The same test fails outside the US: R² 0.38 across 26 markets, a 75.4 kWh/stall/day intercept, and implied power from 24 to 77 kW on near-identical 200–250 kW hardware. A non-zero intercept is not physical, because energy must stay proportional to occupied time. Use the US state ranking with high confidence. Treat the international ranking as provisional. Tesla also prices AE and LT, where no matched Supercharger sites exist to check against. Cross-check run October 1, 2026 against measured occupancy of August 3, 2026.
